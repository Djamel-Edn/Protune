Write the body of a cover letter, as an array of paragraphs. Requirements:

- Between four and five paragraphs. No salutation, no sign-off, no address block —
  paragraphs only.
- Open on something specific to this company or role. Never open with
  "I am writing to apply for" or any variation of it.
- Every claim must be traceable to the CV above. If the CV does not evidence something
  the posting asks for, write around it — do not invent experience, employers or results.
- Prefer concrete detail over adjectives: a named project and what it achieved beats
  "passionate" and "motivated".
- Mirror the posting's own vocabulary where it is honest to do so, so the letter reads as
  an answer to this posting rather than a template.
